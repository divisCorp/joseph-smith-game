How it works: each character's original painting is cut into parts (head, torso, coat skirt, arms, forearms, thighs, shins, boots) and animated on one shared skeleton. Proportions and faces never change; hidden cloth under the arms is repainted from the same painting; 2-bone IK keeps planted feet locked to the ground (watch the tick marks). Space = play/pause, ←/→ = step a frame while paused.
